# Key Insights

## Customer & Revenue

- Flowly has 10,000 customers across 8 countries and 4 customer segments.
- Total paid revenue is approximately $41.05M.
- The Professional plan generated the largest share of paid revenue.

## Churn

- Overall churn rate is 13.21%.
- Churn varies across subscription plans.
- Free customers have the highest observed churn rate among the plans.
- Enterprise customers have the lowest observed churn rate.

## Engagement

- Low-engagement customers show substantially higher churn than high-engagement customers.
- High-engagement customers have an observed churn rate of approximately 3.39%.
- Low-engagement customers have an observed churn rate of approximately 26.74%.

## Feature Adoption

The most widely adopted features are:

1. Projects & Tasks
2. Team Collaboration
3. Collaborative Documents
4. Design Studio
5. Analytics
6. Integrations

## Support

Support ticket volume, resolution time, and satisfaction scores are very similar between active and churned customers in this synthetic dataset. Therefore, the analysis does not show a strong observed relationship between support experience and churn.

## Cohort Analysis

Cohort analysis was used to examine customer activity retention over time based on signup month.
